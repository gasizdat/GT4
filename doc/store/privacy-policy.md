## The short version

Genealogy Tree runs entirely on your device. It has no account system, no server,
and no analytics. Nothing you enter into it is sent to us, because there is
nowhere for it to be sent to — we operate no service that receives it.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything the app keeps lives in ordinary folders on your own computer, under
your own user account:

- **Your genealogy projects** — the names, dates, relationships, biographies,
  photos and attached documents you enter — are saved as `.gt4` files in your
  Documents folder, under `GT4`. Each project is one file. You can copy, move,
  back up or delete it like any other document.
- **Working copies and restore points** of an open project are kept in the app's
  own data folder while it is in use.
- **Your settings** — language, theme, text size, date format, name display order
  — are kept in a small configuration file in the same folder.
- **Error logs**, if the app fails, are written to `GT4\CrashLogs` in your
  application data folder. They are never uploaded; they exist so you can read
  them or send them to us yourself if you choose to report a problem.

The developer has no access to any of it.

## What the app sends over the network

Genealogy Tree works offline. It makes a network request in exactly two
situations, both of which you start yourself:

1. You reference an image by web address inside a biography, and the app fetches
   that image so it can display it.
2. You click a web link inside a biography, and the app hands it to your browser.

In both cases the request goes to the site you named, not to us, and it carries
nothing about your family data beyond the address you supplied. If you never put
a web address into a biography, the app never makes a network request at all.

## What the app does not do

- No account, sign-in or registration.
- No cloud storage or synchronisation.
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting service, or usage measurement.
- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
- No sale or sharing of personal information — there is none to share, because
  none of it reaches us.
- No location, contacts, microphone or camera access.

## Files you choose to open

When you import a GEDCOM file, attach a document or pick a photo, Windows shows
you its own file picker and the app receives only the file you selected. It does
not browse or index the rest of your disk.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects nothing from anyone, children
included.

## Your control

Deleting a project file deletes that project's data. Uninstalling the app leaves
your project files in your Documents folder, so uninstalling never destroys your
research; delete them yourself if you want them gone. Because nothing is stored
remotely, there is no data of yours for us to retrieve, correct or erase on
request.
