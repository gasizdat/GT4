Biography

The most private of the three, and the one who most disliked being away from Haworth. She lasted three months at Roe Head and less than a year in Brussels. At home she kept the household running, walked the moors, and wrote — with Anne — the long Gondal saga the two of them never stopped adding to.

1801 — I have just returned from a visit to my landlord.

Wuthering Heights appeared in 1847 and baffled its reviewers, who took its violence for coarseness and its author for a man. It is now the more read of the two 1847 novels.

She caught cold at Branwell's funeral in September 1848, refused every doctor offered her, and died three months later.
