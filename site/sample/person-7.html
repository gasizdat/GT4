Biography

Maria's elder sister, known in the household simply as Aunt Branwell. She came from Penzance to nurse her sister and stayed twenty years, raising six children in a cold parsonage she is said never to have stopped comparing unfavourably with Cornwall.

Her legacy paid for the sisters' first book, the 1846 Poems by Currer, Ellis and Acton Bell.
