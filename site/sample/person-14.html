Biography

An Ulsterman from County Antrim, curate at Haworth from 1845. He proposed to Charlotte in December 1852; she refused, her father refused more loudly, and he left the parish. He came back, and they married in June 1854.

Nine months later she was dead. He stayed at Haworth anyway, keeping house for her father until the old man died in 1861, then returned to Ireland, farmed at Banagher, and married again. He outlived every Brontë by forty-five years.
