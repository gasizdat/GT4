Biography

A Cornish merchant's daughter from Penzance, well read and, by her own letters, sharp and funny. She travelled north in 1812 to help at her uncle's school near Leeds, met Patrick within weeks, and married him that December.

Six children followed in seven years. She died at Haworth in September 1821, most likely of cancer, when her youngest was barely a year old.
