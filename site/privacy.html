Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

The short version

Genealogy Tree runs entirely on your device. It has no account system, no server and no analytics. Nothing you enter into it is sent to us, because there is nowhere for it to be sent to: we operate no service that receives it.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app keeps lives on your own device:

  • Your genealogy projects (the names, dates, relationships, biographies, photos and attached documents you enter) are saved as .gt4 files in your Documents folder, under GT4. Each project is one file. You can copy, move, back up or delete it like any other document.
  • Working copies and restore points of an open project are kept in the app's own data folder while it is in use.
  • Your settings (language, theme, text size, date format, name display order) are kept in a small configuration file in the same folder.
  • Error logs, if the app fails, are written to a CrashLogs folder in the app's data folder. They are never uploaded; they exist so you can read them or send them to us yourself if you choose to report a problem.

The developer has no access to any of it.

What the app sends over the network

Genealogy Tree works offline. It makes a network request in exactly two situations, both of which you start yourself:

  1. You reference an image by web address inside a biography, and the app fetches that image so it can display it.
  2. You click a web link inside a biography, and the app hands it to your browser.

In both cases the request goes to the site you named, not to us, and it carries nothing about your family data beyond the address you supplied. If you never put a web address into a biography, the app never makes a network request at all.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No cloud storage or synchronisation.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting service, or usage measurement.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information. There is none to share, because none of it reaches us.
  • No location, contacts, microphone or camera access.

Files you choose to open

When you import a GEDCOM file, attach a document or pick a photo, your system shows you its own file picker and the app receives only the file you selected. It does not browse or index the rest of your storage.

Files you export

Exporting a project, whether as GEDCOM, as a .gt4 file, or as an HTML website, creates a file in the app's own cache folder and hands it to your system's share dialog. Where it goes from there is your choice; the app does not upload it anywhere. The copy in the cache folder stays there after sharing. An HTML export contains every person in the project, living people included, so the app asks you to confirm before creating one.

On Android

  • The app declares permission to read your device's shared storage, including the image, audio and video permissions Android groups it under. The app itself reads only its own project files in the GT4 folder of your Documents. Photos and attachments you add come through the system picker, which hands over only the file you choose.
  • The app declares internet access only for the two cases described under "What the app sends over the network".
  • If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include the app's settings and data folder in your backup to your Google account. That backup is run by Android under your account; it is never sent to us.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Your control

Deleting a project file deletes that project's data. Uninstalling the app leaves your project files in your Documents folder, so uninstalling never destroys your research; delete them yourself if you want them gone. Because nothing is stored remotely, there is no data of yours for us to retrieve, correct or erase on request.
